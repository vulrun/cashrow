Cash in, cash out. One row at a time.

The Cashrow mark is a C for cash, cut into three ledger rows. The marigold middle row is the newest entry. The wordmark is Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold: cash in ink, row in cobalt.

Mark

The mark works alone wherever the name is already clear: app icon, favicon, avatar.

  1. A C for cashA thick ring, open to the right like a coin with a slot, reads as C at any size.
  2. Three ledger rowsTwo horizontal cuts turn the ring into rows of a cash book. The cuts stay open down to 16 px.
  3. The newest rowThe middle row is marigold: the entry you just added. It is the only warm colour in the logo.

Rules

Keep the logo clear, legible and in its own colours.

Clear space

Leave at least the height of the marigold row empty on every side.

Minimum size

Horizontal logo: 24 px tall on screen, 16 mm in print. Mark alone: 16 px. Below that, use the favicon.

Backgrounds

Colour logo on white, mist or cobalt 50. Reversed on ink. Mono white on cobalt and photos.

Don’t stretch or squash it.

Don’t recolour the rows.

Don’t put the colour logo on cobalt.

Don’t add shadows or outlines.
